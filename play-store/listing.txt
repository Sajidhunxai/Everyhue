Full description
Every Hue is a personal color and style app. Take a daylight photo, get your seasonal palette, and use it across outfits, shopping, and makeup.

What you can do
• Analyze your colors from a front-facing photo
• Compare two looks
• Match a clothing color to your palette
• Shop shades that fit your season
• Save a wardrobe
• Take the style quiz
• Try hair and makeup colors in Look studio
• Keep history of past analyses
• Add family profiles and ask the stylist
